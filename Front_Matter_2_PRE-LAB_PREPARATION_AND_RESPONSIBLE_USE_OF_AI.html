Overview of Pre-Lab Preparation

Preparing for your laboratory session is an important part of your success in this course. Before attending lab, you are expected to carefully review all assigned materials, including the lab manual, background readings, and any additional instructions provided by your instructor.

Effective reading requires active engagement. This means you should take your time, avoid skimming, and review the material more than once if necessary to fully understand the concepts and procedures. Thoughtful preparation will help you:

  • Work more efficiently during the lab

  • Make accurate observations

  • Better understand the purpose of the experiment

Important Note:
Not all instructors require a pre-lab assignment. Requirements may vary by faculty member, so it is your responsibility to check your course instructions or confirm expectations with your instructor before completing this work.

Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

This course recognizes that students may use AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot) to support learning. When used appropriately, AI can be helpful for:

  • Generating ideas or study questions

  • Clarifying difficult concepts

  • Improving grammar and writing clarity

However, AI is not a replacement for your own thinking or scientific understanding.

Key Expectations

  • AI is not a reliable scientific source

  • AI responses may be incorrect, incomplete, or misleading

  • You are responsible for verifying all information and completing your own work

AI Guidelines for This Lab

You are expected to:

  • Use AI only as a support tool, not as a source of answers

  • Verify all information using textbooks or approved scientific sources

  • Write all responses in your own words

  • Take full responsibility for the accuracy of your work

You are not allowed to:

  • Copy or paste AI-generated content into your assignment

  • Submit AI-generated answers as your own work

  • Use AI instead of reading assigned lab materials

If you use AI to help organize ideas or revise writing, you may acknowledge its use according to your instructor’s guidelines. All scientific content must come from credible sources.

Grading

Each section of the pre-lab assignment is worth 5 points.

Pre-Lab Assignment Sections

Section 1: Essential Ideas of the Lab

Identify the main scientific concept or procedure you will study.

Your response should address:

(a) What is already known about this topic? Include:

  • Key concepts and definitions

  • Important discoveries or developments

  • Any gaps or unanswered questions

(b) Why was this study performed?

  • Explain the purpose of the lab using the title and introduction

Requirements:

  • Write in your own words

  • Use credible sources (e.g., textbooks, lab manual)

  • Cite sources in APA format

Section 2: Lab Objectives

Describe what you will do during the lab.

Examples of objective actions:

  • Observe

  • Measure

  • Analyze

  • Determine

Requirements:

  • Use action-based statements (e.g., “To observe…”)

  • Focus on the procedures you will perform

Section 3: Purpose of the Lab

Explain how lab activities support your understanding of the scientific concept.

This section connects:

  • What you are doing

  • What you are learning

Requirement:

  • Clearly explain this relationship in your own words

Section 4: Hypothesis

(Required for experimental labs only: Labs 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, and 9)

Develop a hypothesis (an educated prediction).

Steps:

  • Identify the independent variable (what you change)

  • Identify the dependent variable (what you measure)

  • Predict the relationship between them

Format:

If [independent variable changes], then [dependent variable will respond], because of [scientific reasoning].

Requirements:

  • Base your hypothesis on scientific knowledge

  • Clearly explain your reasoning

  • Cite any supporting information

Section 5: Questions

Write at least five thoughtful questions about the lab.

Your questions may focus on:

  • Concepts you find confusing

  • Steps in the procedure

  • Expected results

Section 6: References Cited

List all sources in APA (7th edition) format.

CCBC APA Guide:
https://libraryguides.ccbcmd.edu/Citations/apa7

Requirements:

  • Include all sources used

  • Use proper in-text citations

  • Provide a complete reference list

Pre-Lab Checklist (Before Submission)

✔ I carefully read all assigned materials
✔ I confirmed whether this pre-lab is required for my section
✔ I used AI appropriately (if applicable)
✔ I verified all information using reliable sources
✔ I wrote all responses in my own words
✔ I explained how the lab connects to the concept
✔ I included at least five questions
✔ I formatted all references in APA style

Final Note to Students

This pre-lab assignment is designed to help you prepare for the laboratory experience and improve your understanding of scientific concepts. Students who complete the pre-lab thoughtfully are typically more confident, efficient, and successful in the lab.

Remember: success in this course depends on preparation, critical thinking, and academic integrity. Responsible use of AI and independent learning are essential skills for both this class and future scientific work.
